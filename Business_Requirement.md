
Phase 1 — Business requirements

Management wants the dashboard to answer:

Executive performance
What is total revenue?
How many orders were placed?
How many units were sold?
What is the average order value?
How is revenue changing over time?
How are we performing against the sales target?
Sales performance
Which products generate the most revenue?
Which categories are growing/declining?
Which regions perform best?
Which sales representatives perform best?
Customer performance
Who are our highest-value customers?
How many customers are repeat customers?
What percentage of revenue comes from repeat customers?
Time analysis
What are MTD sales?
What is previous-month sales?
What is month-over-month growth?
What is YTD sales?
How are we performing compared with the previous year?
