### Key Business Insights. 
1. **January** recorded the highest Net Sales, while **April** recorded the lowest, indicating considerable variation in monthly sales performance.  
2. Actual Net Sales remained below the monthly sales target throughout the period, indicating a persistent gap between sales performance and business targets.  
3. The business achieved **30.65% of its annual sales target**, resulting in a **144.53K shortfall** against the target.  
4. **North** was the strongest-performing region, generating approximately **29% of total Net Sales**.  
5. **Returns amounted to 1.20K**, equivalent to **1.84% of Gross Sales**, reducing Gross Sales from **65.08K to 63.88K Net Sales**.  
6. **Acme Retail was the highest-grossing customer**, generating 4.8K in Gross Sales.  
7. **The top 5 customers contributed approximately 24.6% of overall Gross Sales**, indicating that a meaningful share of revenue came from the highest-value customers.
